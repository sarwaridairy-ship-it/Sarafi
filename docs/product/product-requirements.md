# SARAFI Product Requirements

SARAFI is a secure multi-tenant operating system for Afghan currency exchange businesses. Its north star is: know exactly where your money is; enter a transaction once and update everything automatically.

## Stage 1-3 scope

- Organization-first tenancy with branches, cashboxes, currencies, and role-scoped access.
- Decimal, immutable, double-entry financial records with atomic idempotent commands.
- Owner, manager, accountant, cashier, viewer, and compliance workflows.
- Authenticated sessions, device visibility, approval segregation, and auditability.
- English, Dari, and Pashto-ready presentation with RTL support.

The UI may simplify accounting terminology, but server-side ledger and authorization invariants are never optional.
