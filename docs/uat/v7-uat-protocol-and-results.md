# SARAFI v7 human UAT protocol and results

Status: protocol prepared; participant results not yet available.

## Participants

- At least 5 daily Sarafi operators.
- At least 2 owners/managers.
- At least 1 accountant.
- At least 1 Hawala payout worker.
- Afghan Dari and Pashto speakers using Android and iPhone, plus tablet/narrow desktop coverage.

## Required tasks

1. Buy and sell currency using Auto, inverse display, stale/manual rate, and required approval.
2. Receive and pay money, create and settle both debt directions, and confirm the receipt wording.
3. Find balances in My Money, explain the 11,000 AFN example, identify a missing-rate exclusion, and switch location.
4. Send a Hawala to one exact destination; prove it is absent from an unrelated branch; acknowledge, ready, capture both Tazkira sides, pay, and open the receipt.
5. Lock and unlock with a supported device/passkey and fallback PIN; test expiry and wrong-PIN lockout.
6. Change business, branch/cashbox, currency/rate, and security settings only with an authorized role.
7. Export and read daily/date-range reports and receipts in the participant's language.

Record task success, completion time, wrong-path taps, help requests, financial misunderstandings, clipped/covered controls, abandonment, and participant wording. Redact identity images and money data from recordings.

## Pass threshold

No P0 financial misunderstanding or cross-scope exposure; at least 90% first-attempt task success for trained daily workflows; no horizontal overflow or covered primary action on target devices; all critical Dari/Pashto wording approved. Failed tasks must be corrected and rerun. The product must not be called premium or production-ready until results are attached here.
